My Dictionary

Manage or delete your My Dictionary data

Use this page to clear saved wordbook data while keeping your account, or to request deletion of your My Dictionary account and its synchronized data.

Clear saved data without deleting your account

To keep your account but clear saved words and review history, open My Dictionary, go to Profile, and choose Clear saved data. This removes saved word entries, including custom meanings, notes, favourites, and learning counts, plus review history from this device. If you are signed in with cloud sync enabled, the app marks those records for removal from cloud sync and sends the deletion when sync next succeeds. Your My Dictionary account and sign-in remain active. To remove one saved word, open it and choose Remove from dictionary.

Local copies on your other devices stay on those devices. Clear saved data on each device too, or uninstall My Dictionary there. If you cannot use the app, contact mydictionay01@gmail.com and describe the saved data you want removed without deleting your account.

Request deletion of your account

Email mydictionay01@gmail.com with the subject “My Dictionary account deletion”. Include the Google account email used with My Dictionary so we can identify the account. We may ask for reasonable proof that you own the account, and aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Never send your password or a verification code. If you no longer have access to the Google account, explain that in your request so we can discuss another way to verify ownership.

What deletion removes

After we verify and process your request, we delete the active My Dictionary account and its associated profile, synchronized saved words, personal meanings, notes, favourites, and review records. In-app deletion also clears that account's local cache from the device where you use it. Deleting a My Dictionary account does not delete your Google account.

Local copies on other devices are stored on those devices and cannot be erased remotely after the account is deleted. Clear My Dictionary's saved data or uninstall the app on each device to remove those copies. Provider-managed logs or backups, where enabled, may remain until the provider's normal retention period expires; see the Privacy Policy.

Delete from the app

If you can still sign in, open My Dictionary, go to Profile, and choose Delete account. Confirm the deletion. The app removes the account and associated synchronized data and clears that account's local cache from the current device. If the app reports that deletion failed, use the email request above.
